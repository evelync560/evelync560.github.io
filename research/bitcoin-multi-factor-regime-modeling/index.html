04 / FORM THE STRATEGY

Apply a different momentum rule in each regime

Price zoneStrategy ruleRationale
LowBuy when EMA20 / EMA50 − 1 crosses above −5%. Exit at ≥0%, ≤−8%, a 12% price stop, or when leaving the zone.Wait for momentum to recover after deep weakness; exit if the recovery fails.
MidBuy when EMA20 / EMA50 − 1 > 4%. Exit below 1% or when leaving the zone.Use a 4% / 1% hysteresis band to confirm the medium-term uptrend and reduce whipsaws.
HighHold BTC only when EMA5 / EMA30 − 1 > 1%; otherwise stay in cash.Stay invested only while short-term momentum remains stronger than the intermediate trend.

Backtest convention: T+1 execution · 0.1% one-way transaction cost · long or cash only.
