01 / DATA & QUESTION

Repair the volume series before testing signals

The original daily Bitcoin volume series contained a visible break around 2022. I replaced it with Coin Metrics spot USD volume and reconstructed unit volume as dollar volume divided by price. Price data runs through August 24, 2026; Coin Metrics dollar-volume coverage ends in May 2026.

Research question: can trend confirmation reduce repeated entries during range-bound markets without giving up most of the upside from major Bitcoin cycles?

02 / TREND FILTERS

Simple trend rules fail in sideways markets

  • 01EMA50 was the strongest standalone EMA specificationbut every simple EMA strategy still produced a maximum drawdown well above 30%.
  • 02Additional confirmation improved the profile, but not enoughrequiring price above EMA200 or improving short-term-holder profitability reduced some false entries, yet 2024–2025 still generated repeated losing trades and drawdowns above 20%.
  • 03Some anti-chop indicators created too many tradesdirectional efficiency ratio and distance from EMA20 produced entry-to-exit win rates near 40%.

Signal comparison

Normalized EMA-spread and MACD-DIF rules produced higher win rates than the higher-turnover filters. The strongest intermediate-regime candidate combined an EMA20/EMA50 spread above 4% with 30-day dollar-volume confirmation.

03 / REGIME FRAMEWORK

Apply signals within the market state they fit

The final framework does not use one rule throughout the full cycle. It first scores the market with three signal families, then applies different exposure rules in low-, intermediate-, and high-valuation regimes. On-chain valuation receives the largest weight because it produced the strongest separation and price relationship.

Low-valuation rule

Enter when the EMA20/EMA50 spread crosses above −5%. Exit when the spread reaches 0% or −8%, a 12% stop triggers, or the market leaves the low-valuation regime. The combined low- and intermediate-regime test produced a +245.1% cumulative return and a 3.45× ending value in the source analysis.

04 / COMBINED RESULT

The main improvement came from drawdown control

The combined strategy did not create a dramatic ending-value advantage. Its stronger contribution was the path: it retained comparable upside while reducing the deepest historical loss shown in the backtest.

05 / WHAT TO VERIFY NEXT

The missing evidence is genuinely out of sample

  • Use walk-forward evaluation or a fixed train-validation split before selecting the 4% spread threshold and regime boundaries.
  • Align the price and Coin Metrics dollar-volume endpoints, and state how the missing final months are handled.
  • Recompute win rate, return, and maximum drawdown from the trade ledger to resolve the 62.5% versus 65.2% discrepancy.
  • Include transaction costs, slippage, and the actual publication time of on-chain signals to test realistic execution.
