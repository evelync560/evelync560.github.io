The perpetual and the A-share trade under different hours, settlement rules, and access constraints. The HIP-3 oracle links the two venues, but T+1 execution, limited shorting, funding, and fragmented liquidity prevent immediate convergence.

03 / HYPERLIQUID MECHANISM

Funding converts oracle premium into a carrying cost

xyz:CXMT is a HIP-3 builder-deployed perpetual operated by trade.xyz. Its oracle carries the external reference price on-chain and updates roughly every three seconds.

PremiumP = IPD / oracle price Approximationhourly funding ≈ premium / 16

After the A-share closes, the external anchor disappears. An EWMA pulls the oracle toward the executable market range; once IPD reaches zero, premium and funding also approach zero.
