Bitcoin Multi-factor Regime modeling

Bitcoin Multi-Factor Regime Modeling

A daily allocation framework that combines market-state classification with technical confirmation to reduce drawdowns across Bitcoin cycles.

  • Researched 10+ heterogeneous Bitcoin alpha signals spanning valuation, on-chain positioning, miner economics, investor sentiment, and technical momentum, including MVRV, Holder Profitability, Fear & Greed, Puell Multiple, Hash Ribbons, and trend indicators.
  • Developed a multi-signal regime-classification framework to identify low-, intermediate-, and high-valuation market states and dynamically adjust exposure rather than relying on static buy-and-hold positioning.
  • Backtested the regime-based allocation strategy across multiple market cycles, growing initial capital to 2.82× with 18.6% maximum drawdown, versus 2.68× and 58.4% maximum drawdown for Bitcoin buy-and-hold.
  • Evaluated signal persistence, regime stability, turnover, drawdown behavior, and robustness across parameter specifications, emphasizing the distinction between genuine predictive information and highly correlated indicators.
